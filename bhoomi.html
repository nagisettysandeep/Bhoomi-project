    The Bhoomi method

    Four readings before one recommendation.ఒక సూచన ముందు నాలుగు పఠనాలు.

    01

    The groundనేల

    Every place carries a soil memory — deltaic alluvium, black cotton regur, red sandy loam, laterite. Bhoomi resolves your village to its soil group, then to pH, drainage and moisture-holding behaviour.

    02

    The skyఆకాశం

    Kharif, rabi or summer changes everything. Season decides evaporation, sowing windows and which varieties can finish before the water runs out.

    03

    The waterనీరు

    Canal, borewell, tank or rain-fed. Water reliability is weighted heaviest — a crop that cannot be irrigated at flowering is not a recommendation, it is a risk.

    04

    The plotపొలం

    Holding size shapes labour, mechanisation and market access. Bhoomi scales seed rate, spacing and expected returns to your actual acreage.
